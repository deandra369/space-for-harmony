WHAT IT IS

Coaching in the Quiet of the Evening

Wind Down Wednesdays brings the heart of The Wednesday Reset to your evening, open to everyone. Each week, we gather online for 45 minutes of breathwork, guided self-coaching, and gentle reflection, together.

It’s a chance to let go of the day, hear yourself think, and leave feeling grounded and refreshed.

  • Live, guided, and virtual, so you can join from home
  • A new theme each week
  • Private journaling, with sharing always optional
  • No experience needed
THE HEART OF THE PRACTICE

What Is Heart‑Mind Coherence?

It’s a calm, focused state where your heart, mind, and nervous system work in sync. You reach it through a short breathing practice.

In this state, stress eases, your mind settles, and it becomes easier to hear your own inner wisdom. Every session begins here.

HOW IT WORKS

What Our 45 Minutes Look Like

Bring your journal, settle in, and let the day go.

01FIRST FEW MINUTES

Set Down the Day

A short breathing practice and heart centering help you release the day and settle into heart‑mind coherence, a calm, focused state where your heart and mind work together.

02MOST OF OUR TIME

Guided Self-Coaching

I guide you through questions on the evening’s theme, like self-trust, resilience, or courage. You write privately in your journal and connect with your own inner wisdom.

03LAST FEW MINUTES

Open Reflection

A few voices may share an insight or reflection from the evening. Sharing is always optional.

04CLOSING

A Thought to Rest With

We close with an inspiring thought to carry into your evening and the rest of your week.
